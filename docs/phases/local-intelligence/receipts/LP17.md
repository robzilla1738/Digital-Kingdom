## What changed
- Kit `dk-castle-kit-2.1.0` (2.0.0 still listed in `SUPPORTED_CATALOG_VERSIONS`): six families `dns_registry`,
  `dispatch_house`, `key_registry`, `supply_depot`, `archive`, `outpost`, each with `since_catalog`, origin,
  connection anchors, interior anchor and a one-line silhouette; scene-proposal schema enum + regenerated validators.
- `recordModuleFor()` picks a family only from declared/supported record fields (local-world template stamp,
  provider identity, declared host aliases) and only modules the asset kind allows; `recordProposal()` keeps every
  position/zone of `defaultProposal`. Existing projects keep `defaultProposal` (never re-skinned); newly compiled
  local worlds (`packages/local-world/src/compile.ts`) use `recordProposal`.
- Geometry for the six families at low/medium/high: in footprint/height bounds, ≤4 merged groups, open (leaf-less)
  entrances that reach the interior, roofs-off exposed floors (outpost belvedere deck and posts lift with the roof).
  Bound fixes: outpost front step pulled inside the 2.4 m footprint; archive grille leaves the centre bay open.
- `selectionProxy(spec, roofs)`: full volume with roofs on; 0.24 m plinth under the floor line with roofs off, never
  larger than the footprint. Used by the castle hit proxy.
- `FAMILY_SURFACE` / `familyCapability()`: the inspector shows the registry support level of the surface a family
  depicts ("does not mean this asset is discovered, monitored or protected"; DNS: "no Internet discovery").
- `presentationScene` keeps an unknown (future) module id's asset with its stored footprint/height; the inspector
  labels it a placeholder. Negative test first: `work/m4-red/lp17-unknown-module-red.log` (TypeError on the old code).
- Manifest: all 16 modules, per-LOD triangles/bounds/`geometry_sha256` (`apps/web/src/castle/geometryDigest.ts`,
  SHA-256 over rounded positions+colours), `materials_sha256`, `manifest_sha256`, Apache-2.0, no font/mesh/remote.
- F15 `families` redesign fixture: mixed world with all six families from records, no collisions.
