# LP10 — Deliver compact evidence and check claims (receipt)

Milestone M2, branch `phase/li-m2` (worktree; see LP08 receipt). Builds on LP08 contracts and LP09 job deliveries.

Reused: R01.3 bundles (finding revisions and pinned references), the R05.4 assistant's rule that a claim citing
records it never retrieved is unsupported (generalized here into an availability check over the delivery log), the
LP03 provenance qualifiers, the case export envelope (HMAC manifest + per-section SHA-256) for the packet export, and
the LP09 delivery log (`investigation_deliveries`), which LP10 extends with `packet` and `inspect` channels.

## What changed

- `apps/server/src/investigation/packets.ts` (`EvidencePackets`, `dk.evidence_packet.v1`):
  - Records with exact ref, role (`counter` → `supporting` → `follow_on` → `linked` → `pivot` → `scope_activity`),
    `required` flag (a linked finding's revision-pinned supporting and counter events), digest, obligations, the
    contract revision it was retrieved under, provenance of the delivery (`via`), and untrusted text in its own field.
  - Size budget (4,000–400,000 bytes): the `uncertainty` section (unmet obligations, negative-claim assessability,
    coverage gaps with uncovered spans, non-complete query steps with resumability), the summary and the contract
    reference are never cut; if they alone do not fit, 413. Required records that do not fit are individual
    `omissions.required` entries with an expansion cursor; others are grouped by role with counts; records that are
    no longer stored are `not_stored` omissions. `summary.substitutes_for_records` is always false;
    `complete_for_required` and `complete` are explicit.
  - Non-destructive expansion with a signed cursor bound to the packet id (digest of the full ordered list) and
    project/case; a changed packet refuses the old cursor (409). At least one record per page, so expansion always
    progresses.
  - Exposure records: every record put in a page is logged (channel `packet`, principal) — an access log that changes
    no case, contract, job or conclusion. `GET …/cases/:id/refs?ids=` inspects refs (events exactly as stored, or this
    contract's coverage results) and logs in-scope ones (channel `inspect`).
  - Signed export `dk.evidence_packet_export/1` (`POST …/packet/export`, analyst) with sections `packet`,
    `counterevidence` (every counter record, never cut), `uncertainty`, `omissions`; `POST /api/verify-packet-export`.
- `apps/server/src/investigation/claims.ts` (`ClaimChecker`, `dk.claim_check.v1`): per claim — schema, LP08 contract
  rules, each ref's existence / scope (project/run, contract assets+interval or linked-finding evidence, retrieved
  under a revision with the same scope) / availability (delivered through a job, packet or inspection), coverage refs
  must be coverage results, the structured predicate (`action_observed` against cited delivered in-scope records;
  `action_not_observed` against the complete retrieval, only when the negative requirement is assessable), and
  unsupported-narrative flags (intent/attribution, authorization judgement, absolute/proof language, negation inside
  an observed claim). Support levels: `predicate_checked`, `references_checked_narrative_unverified`,
  `unresolved_documented`, `failed`; `machine_proved` is always false; overall status `rejected` > `draft` (flagged
  narrative; semantic review deferred, EXT05) > `unresolved` > `machine_checked_structured`.
- Conclusion proposals (`POST …/conclusions`, analyst; append-only table with the check result at proposal time;
  `GET …/conclusions`); `POST …/conclusions/check` (viewer, stores nothing).
- `conclusions.ts`: the unresolved draft cites only coverage results (`cov:`) as coverage refs.
- Migration `INVESTIGATION_CONCLUSIONS_MIGRATION`; `hardening.test.ts` rewind updated; test world gained a trusted
  contradiction option (a blocked request contradicted by an independent db effect → counterevidence).

## Limits

- Availability counts only deliveries through contract channels (jobs, packet pages, inspections). A record seen
  through the older generic tools (`dk_search_events`, `dk_get_event`) is not in the delivery log and fails
  availability until it is inspected under the contract.
- The narrative flags are a fixed, conservative word list; they detect common intent/authorization/absolute wording,
  not every unsupported sentence. Prose is never marked supported in any case.
- Predicates cover action observed / not observed (asset, action, optional outcome, actor, interval). Richer
  propositions (sequence, attribution across namespaces) are not mechanically checkable yet and stay
  `references_checked_narrative_unverified`.
- Packet byte accounting is approximate (JSON length of each part plus a fixed reserve).
